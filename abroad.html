Four questions before you look at a single university

What is the total number?

Not tuition. Tuition plus living costs plus travel plus visa plus insurance, times the length of the course, at a realistic exchange rate. Write it down before you fall in love with a campus.

How is it being paid?

Savings, education loan, or both. If it is a loan, the repayment starts whether or not the job does. We will run that maths with you rather than around you.

What happens after the degree?

Post-study work rights differ sharply by country and change with politics. A two-year work window and a six-month one are completely different investments.

Would the same money go further in India?

Sometimes yes. We will say so. A good Indian programme plus the saved capital beats a weak foreign one more often than anyone in this industry admits.

The runway

Twelve to eighteen months, worked backwards

Almost every abroad application that goes wrong went wrong on timing, not on merit.

1

18–12 months out

Country and course shortlist. Budget agreed with the family. IELTS or TOEFL booked with room to retake.

2

12–6 months out

Applications, SOP drafted properly rather than downloaded, LORs requested early, scholarship deadlines mapped.

3

6–0 months out

Offers, loan sanction, proof of funds, visa file, accommodation. This stage is paperwork discipline, nothing else.

Questions parents ask

Straight answers

Is an agent the same as a counsellor?

No. An agent is paid to place students with particular universities. A counsellor should be working out what is right for your child first. We are paid by institutions, we say so openly, and we will name which ones if you ask.

My child's marks are average. Is abroad still possible?

Often yes — many good foreign universities weigh the application as a whole rather than a single percentage. Whether it is worth the money is the harder question, and that is the one we will actually discuss with you.

How early should we start?

Class 11 is not too early for the money conversation. For applications, twelve to eighteen months before the intake. Families who start six months out end up choosing from what is left.

What about education loans?

We will help you understand collateral requirements, moratorium periods and what banks look for, and refer you to lenders. We do not take commission from any lender.
