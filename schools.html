For principals and coordinators

CBSE now requires a career counsellor for every 500 students

The affiliation bye-laws were amended in January 2026, covering Classes 9 to 12. Most schools are still working out how to meet it. We run structured career sessions in Jaipur schools, at no cost to the school.

What changed

Clause 2.4.12, amended January 2026

The requirement came out of a public interest litigation in the Rajasthan High Court on academic stress and the absence of structured career guidance.

01

One per 500

A career counsellor for every 500 students across Classes 9 to 12.

02

Qualification

A graduate or postgraduate degree in Humanities, Science, Social Sciences, Management, Education or Technology.

03

Hub and spoke

Larger schools may mentor nearby schools, so resources can be shared rather than duplicated.

We are not a substitute for reading the circular. If you are assessing how your school meets the requirement, confirm the position directly with the CBSE Regional Office, Ajmer — and we are happy to share what we have understood from it.

The session

What we actually do in a school

Group session — 45 minutes

  • How stream and course choices shape the next ten years
  • Entrance exams that apply to this cohort, plainly explained
  • What colleges actually cost, all in, over the full course
  • Questions, openly — usually the most useful part

Individual assessment

  • A written assessment form, filled with parental consent
  • Interests, strengths, marks and family budget
  • A personal report for each student who wants one
  • Follow-up with the family after board results

Terms, stated plainly

No cost to the school. No selling in your classroom.

We do not charge the school and we do not charge your students or their parents. We are paid by colleges when a student we have counselled later chooses to enrol with them (how we are paid) — and that conversation happens with the family, after board results, away from your campus.

No commercial activity on school premises, no fees collected on site, and nothing shared with any institution without written parental consent.

Your students get guidance. Your school gets a record of it. Nothing is sold in your building.

Questions schools ask

Straight answers

Does an external counsellor satisfy the CBSE requirement?

That is a question for CBSE, not for us, and we will not tell you it does. The circular reads as an instruction to appoint. What we can offer is structured, documented career guidance for your students while you work out your own compliance position.

Will you collect money from our students?

No. Not on your premises, not afterwards, not at any stage. If anyone representing us asks a parent for money, tell us and we will end it.

What happens to student data?

Assessment forms are held by our counselling team with signed parental consent. Nothing goes to any college unless the family asks us to apply. Any parent can ask us to delete their child's record and we will.

Can we see the material first?

Yes. Ask and we will send the session outline and a blank assessment form before you commit to anything.
