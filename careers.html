Help families make one of the biggest decisions they will make

Campuscout is a small career guidance practice in Jaipur. If you care about giving students honest advice — including advice that costs us money — we would like to hear from you.

The work

What the job actually involves

Everything on this website is a promise someone on the team has to keep. This is what that looks like day to day.

01

In schools

Running structured career sessions for Classes 9 to 12, explaining streams, entrance exams and real college costs plainly, and answering the questions students are usually too shy to ask.

02

With families

Sitting with a student and their parents, understanding marks, interests and budget, and writing a shortlist they can keep — across safe, likely and stretch options.

03

Through to admission

Tracking deadlines, counselling rounds and documents with each family until their child has a seat, not until they have a report.

04

Keeping it accurate

Checking fees, exam dates and admission rules against official sources, because families act on what we tell them.

What matters to us

The way we work is not negotiable

  • You tell every family how we are paid, in the first conversation
  • You never take money from a student or parent for India or school work
  • You put a college on a shortlist because it fits, whether or not it pays us
  • Nothing is shared with any institution without written parental consent
  • You say “I do not know, let me check” rather than guess

If the right college for a child pays us nothing, you will still tell the family it is the right college.
