About

A counsellor, not a call centre

Campuscout is a career guidance practice in Jaipur. We work with Class 11 and 12 students and their parents on college decisions — in India and abroad.

Why this exists

The numbers are not subtle

  • Around one in ten Indian students ever receives professional career guidance
  • The counsellor-to-student ratio in India is roughly 1:3,000. The global norm is 1:250
  • Most families make a ten to twenty lakh rupee decision on hearsay

A decision this size deserves an hour of someone's undivided attention. That is the whole idea.

Who you will be speaking to

A dedicated career counsellor

Every family works with one experienced counsellor who knows Classes 9 to 12 career choices inside out, from streams and entrance exams to colleges and courses. Sessions began after CBSE amended its affiliation bye-laws in January 2026 to require a career counsellor in every affiliated school.

The same person runs your sessions, writes your report and follows up afterwards, so you never have to explain your child's situation twice.

How we are paid

Colleges pay us. For India admissions, families never do.

When a student we have counselled chooses to enrol at a college we work with, that college pays us a referral fee. That is how our school and India work is paid for. The one exception is funded study-abroad support, which colleges do not pay us for, so we charge for it directly and say so before we start.

We tell every family this at the first meeting, because the alternative is that they find out later and reasonably wonder what else we did not mention. Ask us which colleges we have arrangements with and we will tell you.

It also means shortlists include colleges that pay us nothing, because a family that recommends us to three other families is worth far more than one fee.

If the right college for your child pays us nothing, we will still tell you it is the right college.

What we will not do

Four promises, and you can hold us to them

01

We will not charge for India admissions

No registration fee, no assessment fee, no report fee, no fee at admission. Funded study-abroad support is the only paid service, and its fee is agreed in writing first. If anyone claiming to represent us asks for money otherwise, tell us.

02

We will not guarantee a seat

Nobody honest can. Anyone promising a guaranteed admission for a payment is describing something you should walk away from.

03

We will not pass on your details

Nothing goes to any college unless you ask us to apply. Ask us to delete your record and we will, at no cost.

04

We will not hide the conflict

You will hear how we are paid in the first conversation, not the last. Every time.

Questions parents ask

Straight answers

Is this really free, or is there a fee later?

School counselling and India admissions are free throughout. No registration fee, no assessment fee, no report fee, no fee at admission. Funded study-abroad support is the one paid service, and its fee is agreed in writing before any work starts.

Will you push my child towards a particular college?

You will get a shortlist with reasons attached, including options that pay us nothing. Ask which colleges we have an arrangement with and we will tell you.

My child has not chosen a stream yet. Too early?

Class 11 is the right time, not too early. Stream selection closes more doors than any other decision, and it is usually made on the least information.

What happens to our details?

They stay with our counselling team, held with signed parental consent. Nothing is shared with any institution unless you ask us to apply, and you can have everything deleted at any time.

Do you work with schools?

Yes. Principals and coordinators can read what a school session involves on the For schools page.
